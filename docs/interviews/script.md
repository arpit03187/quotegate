# QuoteGate interview script

30 minutes. Owner, GM, or the person who currently approves estimates. Record with permission. Do not pitch until minute 22.

## Open (2 min)

- We’re building a phone approval layer for quotes and change orders — the agent drafts, you send.
- Not selling today. Trying to understand the current exception path.
- OK to record / quote anonymously?

## Current path (10 min)

1. Walk me through the last quote that left the truck. Who wrote it, in what app, who saw it before the customer?
2. When a homeowner asks for extra work mid-job, what happens between “sure” and a signed number?
3. Who is allowed to send a price today? Is that a written rule or a tribal one?
4. What is the dollar threshold where *you* want to see it first?
5. On what device do you actually approve — truck phone, office desktop, Slack, text from the tech?

## Cost of a wrong send (8 min)

6. Tell me about a quote or change order that went out wrong. What did it cost (margin, callback, review, relationship)?
7. How often does a tech send before the pricebook is right — weekly, monthly, rarely?
8. How long from site visit to the customer seeing a number? What would 20 minutes instead of next morning be worth?
9. Do you already use Avoca, an answering service, PandaDoc, or quote follow-up sequences? Where does that break?

## HITL design (6 min)

10. If a draft showed up on your lock screen with scope, price, and a customer SMS, would you swipe, edit a line, or still open Jobber / Housecall Pro?
11. What must never auto-send (discounts, replacements, change orders, anything over $X, new customers)?
12. Would you pay per approved send, a monthly platform fee, or both? Ballpark?

## Close (4 min)

13. Design partnership: 90 days, $0, 20 quotes/week, weekly 30-min review, we live in TestFlight. Interested?
14. Who else should we talk to (another owner, a GM, a tech who currently types quotes)?
15. OK to follow up with an LOI this week?

## Scoring (after the call)

Score 1–5 each: pain intensity, HITL necessity, mobile-native, Jobber/HCP fit, design-partner energy, willingness to pay. **Advance to LOI if total ≥ 22 and HITL ≥ 4.**
